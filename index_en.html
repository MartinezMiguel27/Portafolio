Dashboard Narrative Walkthrough

Interactive Guide • Use the image selector on the left

Follow this guide to understand the business logic of each view in the dashboard.

1

General Summary (Overview)

The primary goal of this dashboard is to consolidate scattered information from multiple systems to provide executive management with rigorous financial and contract control, mitigating cost overrun risks and accelerating decisions.

This page displays a general summary of the contracting process status. We visualize the performance of an annual contracting plan, showing budget status, physical progress, and active contracts at a glance.

2

Strategy Department

Within the organization, the strategy department reviews the scope and metrics requested by user departments for each contract request.

In this view, we track bottlenecks in the initial review phase. The funnel chart and cycle times help identify if a request is stalled due to technical definitions or cost estimation delays, triggering immediate alerts for corrective actions.

3

Services Contracting & Amendments

Subsequent tabs detail the services contracting status and contract amendment logs. This tracking is vital to trace scope, time, or cost modifications relative to original contracts.

The visualization compares the initial committed amount versus the current amount post-amendment, detecting significant variances that impact the budget (ACPP).

4

Procurement, Materials & Budget Adjustments

Lastly, the procurement and budget movement sheets consolidate logistical data extracted from SAP MM with financial execution.

Here, management can analyze projected vs. actual data: material billing deliveries and fund transfers executed to cover commitments, completing the integrated control cycle.
